# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

Tab Out (the Safari extension and its macOS app, published on the Mac App Store as "Tab Out: Tab Manager & Cleaner") does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

## What Tab Out accesses

- **Your open tabs.** To show your tabs grouped by website, Tab Out reads the titles and URLs of tabs open in Safari through the browser's `tabs` API. This happens on your Mac, only while the Tab Out new tab page is open.
- **Saved for later.** Tabs you choose to save are kept in the browser's local extension storage (`storage.local`) on your Mac. You can remove them at any time, and they are deleted when you remove the extension.

## What Tab Out does not do

- No accounts, sign-up or login
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting SDKs
- No network requests: tab data never leaves your device
- No data is sold or shared with anyone

## Children

Tab Out does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
